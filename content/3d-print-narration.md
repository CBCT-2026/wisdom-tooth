# 3D printed model: 60-second narration script

This is the spoken script that plays while a participant looks at the 3D printed model. No written card goes with the model. The "number" cues match the number stickers on the model. Sources are the same as for the 2D information sheet and the AR web page.

- **English:** 130 words, about 55 to 60 seconds at 130 to 140 words a minute. Flesch-Kincaid grade 2.8.
- **Japanese:** 308 characters, about 55 to 60 seconds at about 320 characters a minute. All kanji are grade 6 or below. Needs a native-speaker check before recording.

The script leaves out these points from the sheet and web page, to fit 60 seconds: the artery and vein, the trigeminal nerve and lingula, the tongue nerve, and the age effect.

## English

This model shows an impacted lower wisdom tooth, number 3. It is stuck under the gum and bone.

A nerve runs in a tube in the jaw bone, number 1. It gives feeling to the lower teeth and gums. Its branch, the chin nerve, number 6, gives feeling to the lower lip and chin.

The tooth roots wrap around the nerve, number 2. So taking the tooth out can press, stretch or cut the nerve.

Then the lip, chin, teeth or gums on that side may feel numb, tingle or hurt. Most often, the feeling comes back within weeks to months. Sometimes it never fully comes back.

For teeth like this, about 7 in 100 people had nerve damage. About 1 in 100 had damage that did not go away.

## Japanese

このモデルは、うまっている下の親知らず、番号3です。歯ぐきと骨の下にあります。

下あごの骨の中の管を、神経が通っています。番号1です。この神経は、下の歯と歯ぐきに感覚を伝えます。その枝のおとがい神経、番号6は、下くちびるとあごに感覚を伝えます。

親知らずの根は、神経に巻き付いています。番号2です。そのため、歯をぬくと、神経がおされたり、のびたり、切れたりすることがあります。

すると、同じ側の下くちびる、あご、歯、歯ぐきが、しびれたり、いたんだりします。多くは、数週間から数か月で感覚がもどりますが、完全にはもどらないこともあります。

このような親知らずでは、100人中約7人に神経の傷が起こり、約1人は治りませんでした。
