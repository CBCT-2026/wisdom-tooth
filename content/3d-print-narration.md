# 3D printed model and narrated video: 45-second narration script

This is the spoken script that plays while a participant looks at the 3D printed model. The narrated video uses the same script, with subtitles. No written card and no stickers go with the model, as in a clinic. The script points to parts by colour and position (the red nerve). Sources are the same as for the 2D information sheet and the AR web page.

- **English:** 107 words, 45 seconds at a comfortable pace of about 145 words a minute with short pauses. Flesch-Kincaid grade 2.7.
- **Japanese:** 239 characters, about 45 seconds at about 310 characters a minute. All kanji are grade 6 or below. Needs a native-speaker check before recording.

To fit 45 seconds at a comfortable pace, the script leaves out these points from the sheet and web page: the tube in the bone, the artery and vein, the name of the chin nerve, the tongue nerve, the list of symptoms, how long recovery takes, and the age effect. The last sentence recommends a specialist check without saying it lowers the risk (see the "Specialist referral and reference check" review). "The nerve can be pressed or cut" follows suggested reference fix 3 from that review.

## English

This is an impacted lower wisdom tooth. Its roots wrap around a nerve. Taking the tooth out can damage the nerve and make your lip and chin numb.

The red part is the nerve. It gives feeling to the lower teeth, gums, lip and chin.

As the tooth comes out, the nerve can be pressed or cut. The feeling often comes back, but sometimes it never does.

Rates vary, but about 7 in 100 may get nerve damage. 1 in 100 may have damage that does not go away.

For a tooth like this, it is a good idea to see a specialist (an oral surgeon) first.

## Japanese

これは、うまっている下の親知らずです。根が神経に巻き付いています。歯をぬくと、神経が傷つき、くちびるやあごがしびれることがあります。

赤い部分が神経です。下の歯、歯ぐき、くちびる、あごに感覚を伝えます。

ぬくときに、神経がおされたり切れたりすることがあります。多くは感覚がもどりますが、もどらないこともあります。

差はありますが、100人中約7人に神経の傷が起こるかもしれません。約1人は治らないかもしれません。

このような親知らずは、まず口くう外科の専門医にみてもらうとよいでしょう。
